Good to know

How long does a keratin treatment last?

Typically 3 to 5 months, depending on your hair type and care routine. Sulfate-free shampoo helps it last. Most clients come in 2 to 3 times a year.

What's the difference between Keratin Complex and ProAddiction?

Keratin Complex is the gold-standard keratin treatment and dramatically reduces frizz and curl for 3 to 5 months. ProAddiction is a newer, formaldehyde-free system that lasts up to 5 months and leaves an extraordinary gloss. Both cut daily styling time significantly.

Will it make my hair completely straight?

Not necessarily, and that's by design. Smoothing treatments are about eliminating frizz and making hair manageable. They soften curl and wave, and how straight you go depends on your natural texture and the formula. If you want to keep some wave, we can do that.

How much does keratin cost?

Smoothing is priced at a free consultation because length, thickness and texture change the amount of product and time needed. You get an exact quote before anything starts.
