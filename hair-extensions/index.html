Good to know

Are extensions right for my hair?

Extensions add length, volume or both and work on most hair types. At a free consultation Marina checks your natural hair's health, texture and thickness to choose the best method and build a custom plan.

How much do hair extensions cost?

Hand-tied extensions start at $175 per row, and most looks need more than one row. Because every install is custom, you get an exact quote at your free consultation.

How long do weft extensions last?

Hand-sewn wefts typically go 6 to 8 weeks before a move-up appointment ($175), where they're repositioned as your hair grows. With proper care, the hair itself can be reused for multiple installations.

Which extension methods do you offer?

We install hand-tied and sewn-in weft extensions, and we style and blend clip-ins. Marina will recommend the best method for your hair at your consultation.
