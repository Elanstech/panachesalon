Questions

Good to know

How much does balayage cost at NYC Panache?

Partial balayage starts at $200, half head at $250 and full head at $350. Highlights start at $150 for face framing. Your final price depends on length, thickness and the look you want, and you get an exact quote at a free consultation.

How long does a balayage or highlight appointment take?

Usually 2.5 to 4 hours, depending on your hair length, thickness and the result you want. A toner and blowout are included in every color service.

What's the difference between balayage and highlights?

Traditional highlights use foils for even, uniform streaks of color. Balayage is painted freehand for softer, more natural dimension with no harsh grow-out lines. Marina will recommend the right technique at your consultation.

What is air-touch balayage?

Air-touch is a balayage technique where a blow-dryer separates the finest hairs before painting, leaving them uncolored. The result is hyper-natural, very dimensional color. It's our signature technique.

How often do I need a touch-up?

Balayage and highlights can usually go 8 to 12 weeks between appointments thanks to the soft, blended grow-out. Single-process color and root touch-ups are typically every 4 to 6 weeks.
