About Marina and NYC Panache Salon

Marina Iskiyaeva has been doing hair for more than 25 years. NYC Panache is her salon in Forest Hills, Queens.

Book onlineCall (718) 575-0532

Our story

Marina opened NYC Panache Salon so clients in Forest Hills could get careful, unhurried work close to home. She does cuts, color, balayage, smoothing treatments and extensions, and the salon also offers nails, waxing and makeup, so you can take care of everything in one visit.

Color is what Marina is best known for. Air-touch balayage is her signature technique, and color corrections are a specialty. Many of her clients have been coming to her for years.

Every first visit starts with a conversation about your hair history and what you want. You hear your price before anything begins, and nothing is rushed.
