Hair & Color

Balayage and highlight services typically take 2.5 to 4 hours depending on your hair length, thickness, and the desired result. We never rush — quality takes time. A toner and blowout are included in every color service.

Traditional highlights use foils and create uniform, even streaks of color. Balayage is a freehand painting technique that produces softer, more natural-looking dimension with no harsh grow-out lines. During your consultation, Marina will recommend which technique best suits your hair goals.

Air-touch is an advanced balayage variation where a blow-dryer is used to separate fine hairs before painting. This isolates shorter and thinner strands, leaving them uncolored for a hyper-natural, incredibly dimensional result. It's our signature technique and ideal for anyone who wants sun-kissed depth without harsh lines.

For single-process color or root touchups, we typically recommend every 4–6 weeks. Balayage and highlights can be stretched longer — usually 8–12 weeks — thanks to the soft, blended grow-out.

Absolutely. Color corrections are one of Marina's specialties. Whether you have uneven color, banding, brassiness, or damage from a previous salon, we'll assess the situation during a consultation and create a custom correction plan. Please note that corrections may require multiple sessions.

Smoothing Treatments

Keratin treatments typically last 3–5 months depending on your hair type and care routine. We recommend using sulfate-free shampoos to extend the life of your treatment. Most clients come in 2–3 times per year.

Keratin Complex is the gold-standard keratin treatment — it dramatically reduces frizz and curl for 3–5 months. ProAddiction is our newer, next-generation system that is formaldehyde-free, lasts up to 5 months, and leaves an extraordinary gloss. Both will cut your daily styling time significantly. Marina can recommend the best option during your consultation.

Extensions & Up-Dos

Extensions are great for adding length, volume, or both. They work on most hair types, but the best way to find out is through a free consultation. Marina will assess your natural hair's health, texture, and thickness to determine the best extension method and create a custom plan.

Every extension installation is entirely custom — tailored to your hair length, texture, thickness, and desired result. The amount of hair needed, the method used, and the time involved vary significantly from person to person. A consultation ensures you receive an accurate, personalized quote with no surprises. Hand-tied extensions start at $175 per row.

Hand-sewn weft extensions typically last 6–8 weeks before needing a move-up appointment ($175), where the wefts are repositioned as your natural hair grows. With proper care, the hair itself can be reused for multiple installations.

Nails & Waxing

We offer a full range of nail services including classic manicures ($15), pedicures, gel manicures ($45), gel pedicures ($55), French manicures, gel mani-pedi combo ($100), silk nails, dip powder, and polish changes. Every service is delivered with the same meticulous care and attention to detail that defines Panache.

We offer facial waxing (eyebrow $15, upper lip $10, chin $10, full face $45), body waxing (underarm, arms, legs, stomach, chest, back), and bikini ($20) and Brazilian waxing ($45). We also offer threading for brows and full face, plus a full body wax for $200. Our technique is gentle, precise, and designed for long-lasting results with minimal discomfort.

For best results, we recommend waxing every 3–4 weeks. This allows hair to grow to the ideal length for effective removal and helps achieve smoother, longer-lasting results over time as the hair grows back finer.
