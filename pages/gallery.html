What Goes Into Each Look

Behind every transformation is a process. Here's what you can expect from our most popular services.

Color
Signature Service

Balayage & Air-Touch

Balayage is a freehand painting technique that creates soft, graduated color with no harsh grow-out lines. Our air-touch variation takes it further — using a blow-dryer to separate fine hair before painting, giving an even more natural, dimensional finish that moves beautifully in light.

  • Starts at $350 — Full Head
  • 2.5 – 4 hours depending on length
  • Works on all hair types & base colors
  • Toner + blowout included

Smoothing
Transformation Treatment

Keratin & ProAddiction Smoothing

Our smoothing treatments infuse the hair shaft with keratin protein to eliminate frizz, reduce curl, and add an extraordinary gloss. The ProAddiction system is our newest offering — a formaldehyde-free formula that lasts up to 5 months and dramatically cuts your daily styling time.

  • Priced by consultation
  • Results last 3 – 5 months
  • Works on most textures and curl patterns
  • Use sulfate-free shampoo to extend
