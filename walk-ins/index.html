More about Marina

Good to know

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

You can walk in. We take walk-ins and appointments. If you want a specific time or stylist, book online through Vagaro or call (718) 575-0532.

Is the salon open today?

We are open Tuesday through Saturday from 10 AM to 7 PM and Sunday from 10 AM to 5 PM. We are closed on Monday.

Are you open on Sunday?

Yes. NYC Panache Salon is open Sunday from 10 AM to 5 PM, and walk-ins are welcome.

Can I get a same-day haircut or blowout?

Call (718) 575-0532 and we'll tell you the soonest open chair today, or just come by during open hours.

How much is a haircut or blowout?

A wash and cut is $55, a blowout is $45, and a shampoo, cut and blowdry is $75. A men's or barber cut is $40.

Where is the salon, and how do I get there?

We're on the second floor at 107-12 71st Road in Forest Hills, between Queens Boulevard and Austin Street. It is about a 3-minute walk from the Forest Hills-71 Av subway station (E, F, M and R trains) and the Forest Hills LIRR station.

Is there parking nearby?

Yes. There is metered street parking on 71st Road and the surrounding streets, and there are parking garages a short walk away on Queens Boulevard.

What forms of payment do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash. Payment is collected at the end of your service.
