Hair and color

How long does a balayage or highlight appointment take?

Usually 2.5 to 4 hours, depending on your hair length, thickness and the result you want. A toner and blowout are included in every color service.

What's the difference between balayage and highlights?

Traditional highlights use foils for even, uniform streaks of color. Balayage is painted freehand for softer, more natural dimension with no harsh grow-out lines. Marina will recommend the right technique at your consultation.

What is air-touch balayage?

Air-touch is a balayage technique where a blow-dryer separates the finest hairs before painting, leaving them uncolored. The result is very natural, dimensional color. It's our signature technique.

How often should I touch up my roots?

For single-process color and root touch-ups, every 4 to 6 weeks. Balayage and highlights can usually go 8 to 12 weeks thanks to the soft, blended grow-out.

Can you do color corrections?

Yes. Color corrections are one of Marina's specialties. For uneven color, banding, brassiness or damage from a previous service, we assess your hair at a consultation and build a correction plan. Corrections can take more than one session.

What products do you use?

We use professional, salon-exclusive color, smoothing and styling products chosen for performance and hair health. Ask us about specific products at your visit.

Smoothing

How often should I get a keratin treatment?

Keratin treatments typically last 3 to 5 months, depending on your hair type and care routine. Sulfate-free shampoo helps them last. Most clients come in 2 to 3 times a year.

What's the difference between Keratin Complex and ProAddiction?

Keratin Complex is the gold-standard keratin treatment and greatly reduces frizz and curl for 3 to 5 months. ProAddiction is a newer, formaldehyde-free system that lasts up to 5 months and leaves a strong gloss. Both cut daily styling time. Marina can recommend the best option at your consultation.

Can I color my hair after a smoothing treatment?

Yes, but timing matters. We recommend coloring first, or waiting at least 2 weeks after smoothing before you color. We'll help you schedule both.

Will keratin make my hair completely straight?

Not necessarily. Keratin is mainly about removing frizz and making hair manageable. It softens curl and wave, and how straight you go depends on your natural texture and the formula. If you want to keep some wave, we can do that.

Extensions and updos

Are extensions right for me?

Extensions add length, volume or both and work on most hair types. At a free consultation Marina checks your hair's health, texture and thickness to choose the best method and build a plan.

Why are extensions priced by consultation?

Every install is custom. The amount of hair, the method and the time vary from person to person, so a consultation gets you an accurate quote with no surprises. Hand-tied extensions start at $175 per row.

How long do weft extensions last?

Wefts typically go 6 to 8 weeks before a move-up appointment ($175), where they're repositioned as your hair grows. With proper care, the hair can be reused for multiple installs.

Do you do bridal and special occasion hair?

Yes. Bridal hair starts at $350, bridal makeup at $250 and special occasion updos at $250. Contact us for a quote for your event.

Nails and waxing

What nail services do you offer?

Classic manicures ($15), pedicures, gel manicures ($45), gel pedicures ($55), French manicures, a gel manicure and pedicure combo ($100), silk nails, dip powder and polish changes.

What waxing services do you offer?

Facial waxing (eyebrow $15, upper lip $10, chin $10, full face $45), body waxing (underarm, arms, legs, stomach, chest and back), bikini ($20) and Brazilian ($45). We also offer brow threading and a full body wax for $200.

How often should I get waxed?

Every 3 to 4 weeks works best. That lets hair grow to the right length for removal, and results get smoother over time.
